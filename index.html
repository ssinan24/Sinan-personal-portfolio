About Me

My journey and passion for technology

Hello! I'm Mohammed Sinan, a self-taught developer currently pursuing my BSc in Computer Science. My coding journey started with a deep curiosity for how things work on the internet, which quickly blossomed into a full-fledged passion for software engineering.

I specialize in the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) and the .NET ecosystem. Whether I'm building a sleek frontend interface or architecting a robust backend database, I love solving complex problems with clean, efficient code.

Beyond the IDE, I'm an avid tech community member and public speaker. I believe that sharing knowledge is just as important as acquiring it. When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring new tech trends, giving talks, or brainstorming my next big project!
